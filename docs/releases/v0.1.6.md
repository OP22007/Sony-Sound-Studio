# v0.1.6 — Listening history

See how you use your headphones, with everything kept on your computer.

## Listening history

- A new **Listening history** tab shows connected time for today, 7 days, 30 days or all time, with a daily chart, time by listening mode, noise control, device and content, and your recent sessions.
- It opens on the headphone you are using. Other headphones appear only when you pick them in the **Show** menu or choose **All devices**.
- A headphone counts only while Windows also reports its Bluetooth link as up, so time is no longer counted after the headphone has gone away.
- Recording of connected time and mode is on by default. Two optional switches are off by default and apply from the moment you turn them on:
  - **Record apps and sites** saves the playback type (game, music, movie, other) and the app or site name.
  - **Record exact titles and URLs** also saves the full page address including anything after the `?`, the song and artist, and the game's window title. It is never recorded from private browser windows.
- Everything is saved in a local file and nothing is uploaded. **Clear history** deletes it all after a confirmation. Page addresses can contain tokens or search text and are stored as plain text, which is why that switch is separate.
- Playback details, titles and addresses are Windows only. Connected time and mode are recorded on every platform.

## Browser companion 1.0.3

- Sends the address and title of a playing tab for the optional exact-titles switch, only from a tab the browser confirms is not private. The desktop discards them unless you turned the switch on. Reload the companion once in each browser after updating.

## OnePlus

- The diagnostics log now records spatial audio details for OnePlus earbuds: the path used, the earbuds' capability bytes, the change command and the raw readbacks, including failed readbacks. This helps investigate reports that the effect is not audible. Nord Buds 3 Pro owner logs show the earbuds acknowledging and reading back spatial audio changes, so the setting itself is applied.
- The App settings privacy text now says history stays on this computer.

## Verification and limitations

Windows, macOS and Linux CI passed, including 193 native tests and 87 browser tests. Titles, addresses, game detection and the Bluetooth-link check are covered by unit tests and mocks, and have not been exercised on real headphones and games. The time shown is connected time per mode and does not prove audio was playing. Day boundaries use your timezone offset when you view them, so a range that crosses a daylight-saving change can be off by an hour at its edge. Track titles come only from players that publish them to Windows; VLC's local connection does not provide titles.

Windows WH-1000XM6 firmware 3.1.5 remains the hardware-confirmed Sony baseline; OnePlus support is experimental. macOS control connections remain disabled and Linux control support needs hardware testing. Automatic listening is Windows-only.

## Updating

From **v0.1.2** or later, open **App settings → App updates → Check for updates**, then choose **Install & restart**. DEB/RPM users install the new package manually; Linux in-app updates require AppImage. Manual downloads include the Windows x64 setup, macOS Apple Silicon and Intel DMGs, and Linux x64 AppImage, DEB and RPM packages, with SHA-256 checksums. Builds are not Apple-signed or notarized, and the Windows installer has no Authenticode publisher signature.

Report issues through the repository's tester-feedback template with your OS, app version and headphone model/firmware.
